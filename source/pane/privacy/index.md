---
title: pane privacy policy
---
pane is a personal tool used only by its owner. It requests read-only access to the owner's Google Drive in order to build a private search index. That index stays on the owner's own computer. pane does not modify Drive, does not share data with anyone, and does not sell or transfer data to third parties. Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's security settings, under third-party connections.
